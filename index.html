WELCOME, HERO!

This is Azure Academy, a free, gamified trainer for the Microsoft AZ-900 (Azure Fundamentals) exam — daily quests, XP and loot instead of a plain flashcard deck.

  • 370+ quiz questions on Easy, Normal and Heroic, plus open-answer Azure Labs
  • A daily streak, Mastery ranks and a Loot Shop full of gear with real effects
  • A Boss Fight to close each day and Weak Spots for everything you missed
  • Learn: bite-sized lessons in Beginner and Intermediate worlds
  • A timed Practice Exam with a report per domain

It's a personal side project and keeps evolving — expect the odd rough edge.

Progress is saved only in this browser (no account, no server). Make a backup in Hero → Menu → Export backup.
